      ## What the Sessions Are Like

      These are coached group sessions, so you learn the fundamentals
      properly — stance, jab, hooks, defence — and put them to work on the
      bags and pads. The group trains together and the coaching is scaled
      to whoever is in, so it works whether you have boxed before or are
      starting from scratch.

      Sessions are coached by Sayeeda — you can find her on Instagram at
      [@sayeeda.boxing](https://www.instagram.com/sayeeda.boxing).

      ## Class Times

      - **Thursdays, 4–5pm**
      - **Sundays, 4–5pm**

      The sessions are women only. If the times do not work for you, we
      also run ladies' boxfit classes through the week, and private 1-to-1
      and small-group sessions by arrangement.
